常见问题

1. 如何开始训练？

应用启动后会显示默认的网络与热力图。点击左侧的播放按钮（深蓝色圆形内的播放三角）即可开始训练，热力图、误差曲线与训练次数会实时更新；再次点击同一按钮变为暂停。若只想观察单步效果，可点击「单步」按钮，每点击一次执行一轮训练。

2. 如何暂停训练或开启 10× 加速？

播放按钮用于开始与暂停训练。「加速」按钮用于在 1× 与 10× 之间切换：开启后按钮旁显示「10x」标记，每轮训练处理的样本更多、训练速度更快；再次点击恢复 1×。「单步」按钮每点击一次执行一轮训练，适合逐次观察损失与热力图变化。

3. 如何保存热力图图片？

在热力图上长按约 0.5 秒，会弹出「保存到相册」菜单：点「确定」后应用会生成 512×512 的图片并写入系统相册，生成期间显示「生成图像中」，成功时提示「成功保存到相册」；点「取消」则不保存。首次保存时系统会请求照片「仅添加」权限。

4. 相册权限被拒绝怎么办？

拒绝权限不影响训练与可视化功能。可前往 iOS「设置」→「隐私与安全性」→「照片」→「神经网络」，选择「添加照片」；也可在「设置」→「神经网络」→「照片」中修改。修改后回到应用，重新长按热力图保存即可。应用只写入你确认保存的图片，不会读取相册内容。

5. 没有网络可以使用吗？

可以。数据集生成、神经网络训练、热力图渲染与图片导出全部在设备本地完成，核心功能完全离线可用。只有点击参数面板中的信息按钮、用系统 Safari 打开在线使用说明时才需要网络。

6. 如何切换界面语言？

应用界面提供简体中文与英文，语言跟随 iOS 系统设置：可在「设置」→「通用」→「语言与地区」中更改系统语言，也可在「设置」→「神经网络」→「语言」中单独设置本应用语言。应用内没有独立的语言切换按钮。目前保存图片相关的菜单与提示固定为中文（保存到相册 / 取消 / 确定）。

Frequently asked questions

1. How do I start training?

After launch the App shows the default network and heatmap. Tap the play button (a play triangle in a dark blue circle) on the left to start training; the heatmap, loss curve, and iteration count update continuously. Tap the same button again to pause. To advance one iteration at a time, tap the Step button.

2. How do I pause training or enable 10× acceleration?

The play button starts and pauses training. The Accelerate button toggles between 1× and 10×: when enabled, a “10x” label appears next to the button, each training round processes more samples, and training runs faster; tap again to return to 1×. The Step button runs exactly one training round per tap, which is useful for watching the loss and heatmap change step by step.

3. How do I save the heatmap image?

Long-press the heatmap for about 0.5 seconds to show the save menu. Tap “Confirm” (确定) and the App generates a 512×512 image and writes it to your photo library; “Generating image” (生成图像中) appears while it is generated and “Saved to Photos” (成功保存到相册) appears on success. Tap “Cancel” (取消) to skip saving. The first save asks for add-only Photos access. The menu title and these prompts currently use Chinese labels: 保存到相册 (Save to Photos), 取消 (Cancel), 确定 (Confirm).

4. What if Photos permission was denied?

Denying permission does not affect training or visualization. Go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → this App and choose “Add Photos Only”; you can also change it in Settings → this App → Photos. Then return to the App and long-press the heatmap to save again. The App only writes the images you confirm saving and does not read your photo library.

5. Can I use the App without a network connection?

Yes. Dataset generation, neural-network training, heatmap rendering, and image export are all performed on your device, and core features work fully offline. A network connection is needed only when you tap the info button in the settings panel, which opens the online help in a system Safari view.

6. How do I change the interface language?

The App's interface is available in Simplified Chinese and English and follows the iOS system language. You can change the system language in Settings → General → Language & Region, or set the language for this App alone in Settings → this App → Language. There is no separate in-app language switch. The menu and prompts related to saving images currently use Chinese labels only (保存到相册 / 取消 / 确定).

7. How do I report a problem or send feedback?

Email 915505626@qq.com with your device model, iOS version, App version, a description of the issue, and steps to reproduce; screenshots are helpful. Do not send passwords, private keys, private photos, or other sensitive information.
